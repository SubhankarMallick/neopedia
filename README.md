# The Neopedia Foundation

## Objective

Online wikis and educational platforms all suffer from the same chronic ailments: outdated content, aggressive corporate paywalls, or explanations so densely over-engineered that you need a Ph.D. just to understand Class 10 Physics.

Our mission is simple and non-negotiable:

> "Make the reaches of human knowledge extend to every person from every background, every learning level, and every corner of the earth—from remote villages to bustling cities—completely, permanently, and unconditionally free."
> 
> — **The Neopedia Foundation**

## How to Contribute

We rely on top-achieving students, peer tutors, and passionate educators to keep our articles accurate, concise, and accessible.

Ready to write or review a chapter? Check out **`CONTRIBUTING.md`** for our formatting standards, Git workflows, and content guidelines.

## Suggestions & Feedback

Have ideas to improve the platform or spotted an error in a chapter?
