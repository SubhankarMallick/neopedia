Chemical Reactions and Equations

[ 9–10 ]

Chemical reactions are processes in which one or more substances, the reactants, are converted to one or more different substances, the products.

Key Concepts

  • Reactants: The starting materials in a chemical reaction.
  • Products: The substances formed as a result of a chemical reaction.
  • Conservation of Mass: Mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

Important Equations

Mass-energy equivalence equation proposed by Albert Einstein:

Inline math example: $E = mc^2$

Quadratic formula used in stoichiometric calculations:

Block formula example:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Types of Chemical Reactions

  1. Combination Reaction
  2. Decomposition Reaction
  3. Displacement Reaction
  4. Double Displacement Reaction
  5. Oxidation and Reduction (Redox) Reactions
